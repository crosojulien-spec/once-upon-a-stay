# Hotel Kings Court — service DNA

## 1. Review status and use

Research checked: 5 October 2026. Public preparation; operator review pending; not operationally confirmed by the hotel. Prices are reference points, not quotations. Facts, interpretation, proposals and unresolved conflicts remain separate. Examples do not limit the agent to a catalogue.

## 2. Identity and service interpretation

An urban hotel on Republic Square with Italian dining, wellness, executive accommodation and professional event spaces. [K01, K05, K11]

Interpretation: the useful distinction is between the guest's purposes and time constraints, not between a presumed business or leisure personality. The same resources can support a working morning, family meal, restful break or celebration. Avoid prescribing luxury rituals when the guest simply wants efficiency.

## 4. Rooms, sleep and practical comfort

Executive rooms are listed at 25–35 m² with king/twin arrangements, Materasso mattresses and Mühldorfer bedding. A pillow choice is explicitly mentioned, but no exact firmness/filling menu was found. Rooms list Nespresso/tea, minibar, iron, robe and slippers, L'Occitane toiletries, controllable lights/curtains and a bath OR walk-in rain shower. Do not infer both bathing options in a particular room. Lounge/spa access is included for that category. [K02]

Other room categories must be checked individually. Do not grant Executive benefits to a Deluxe booking. Confirm connecting rooms, cots, bed limits, accessibility, quiet-location choices, unscented products and preparation permissions rather than infer them from the hotel's size.

## 6. Wellness, activities and family resources

Dedicated spa page: wellness 10:00–22:00; massage appointments 09:00–21:30, with 09:00–10:00 requiring booking the previous day. Treatments are preferably booked three days ahead; arrive around 15 minutes early. A 90-minute massage includes wellness access. Swimming costumes are required in the pool; sauna/steam use has separate towel/sheet guidance. [K03, K04]

The current brochure uses Nobilis Tilia/Inlight products and describes individually adapted massage. Do not repeat health-effect marketing as medical claims. Treatment suitability, oil/fragrance preference, staffing and total charge need the spa's agreement.

The dedicated page permits accompanied children under 12 only from 10:00–16:00. General-site FAQ text instead suggests a minimum age and different euro tariffs. These conflict: use the dedicated page as the documented working reference, but obtain confirmation before any child-access or price commitment. Child pool access does not imply babysitting. ## 8. Composition possibilities — proposals for staff

| Volunteered guest cue | Known resources | Possible composition | Owner and checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short visit, needs a quiet working hour | Event rooms, business breakfast, AV [K11] | Breakfast coordinated with a small appropriate workspace | Concierge/reception + events: privacy, room, timing, minimum spend, equipment; lounge is not automatically private |

| Celebration without alcohol or public attention | Romantic package, pastry and restaurant [K07, K10] | Ask about discreet cake and non-alcoholic replacement for wine | Reception + F&B: drink options not yet documented, substitution, delivery and price |
| Family wants a simple early meal and some pool time | ADELE cooked dishes and restricted child pool hours [K03, K07] | Coordinate food and a compatible pool window | Reception + restaurant + spa: child's age, actual access policy, supervision, portions and availability |
| Prefers vegetables but has not requested vegan food | Vegetable pizza, ravioli, soup and salads [K07] | Kitchen-selected meal around liked ingredients | Kitchen: distinguish taste from dietary restriction, allergens and charge; do not remove ingredients by assumption |

## 9. Conversation and brief handoff

Explore purpose, fixed commitments, recovery time, food and sleep only as relevant. Ask enough to make a request useful, without selling upgrades. Route ideas to confirmed concierge/reception, then spa/F&B/events/housekeeping as appropriate. Cite the actual room category and inclusion; proposed combinations require a quote. Personalisation refusal suppresses all suggestions.

## 10. Review queue and evidence conflicts

Priority: child spa rule and conflicting FAQ tariffs; exact booked-category benefits; concierge owner; current treatment availability and billing terms. Obtain pillow menu, readable drinks menu, allergy procedures, accessible routes, connecting/cot options, room preparation permissions and package substitution rules. Exclude expired daily offers. No hotel contact, booking or paid action was made.
