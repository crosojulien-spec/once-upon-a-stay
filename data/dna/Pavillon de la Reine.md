# Le Pavillon de la Reine — service DNA

## 1. Review status and use

Research checked: 5 October 2026. Public preparation; operator review pending; no operational hotel confirmation. Published menus and brochures provide resources and reference prices, not availability or permission. Proposed compositions remain open examples.

## 2. Identity and service interpretation

A discreet hotel on Place des Vosges, with a garden setting, lounges, restaurant Anne and a spa. The room collection includes family and apartment configurations. [P01, P02]

Interpretation: a private-home atmosphere can support an unhurried Paris base, but guests may equally prioritise culture, family logistics, food or work. Ask about their purpose instead of prescribing romance or quietness.

## 4. Rooms, sleep and practical comfort

Superior rooms: about 22 m², courtyard-facing, king/queen or twin on request, maximum two occupants. They list individual air conditioning, Nespresso/kettle, Codage amenities, turndown and complimentary minibar water, soda and juice. Flavours and replenishment limits are unknown. [P03]

Two connecting Superior rooms accommodate up to four. A family apartment provides two bedrooms/two bathrooms plus lounge/sofa bed for up to five, with a published 58–90 m² range. Confirm the exact unit/layout. Twin arrangement and baby cot are request-based; the general services page says cot from Deluxe category, so do not promise one in a Superior without checking. [P04, P05]

Adapted rooms are offered on request, without a full accessibility specification. Exact step-free route, bathroom equipment and transfer needs require staff confirmation. No current primary pillow menu or scent-free linen policy was located. [P02]

Spa access is published 07:00–21:00; resident access is complimentary, treatments extra. External access is EUR 40 for the hour preceding a booked treatment. Under-16s require an accompanying adult. Actual access/appointment rules should be confirmed for the family concerned. [P06, P07]

All above from [P07]. The brochure states personalised selection; therapist assessment still governs suitability. Do not make medical claims or infer pregnancy suitability: ask the spa to handle this directly.

Arrive 15 minutes early; cancellation within two hours is charged in full. Brochure gives an extra EUR 30/hour for 07:00–09:00 and 20:00–23:00, and a 100% surcharge after 23:00. This extends beyond normal access hours: appointment feasibility must be confirmed, not presented as standard late opening. [P07]

Bicycles, games, family rooms and concierge coordination are documented. Babysitting, children's spa treatments and pillow choices are not. [P04, P05]

Current restaurant and spa menu prices are useful references, but no price for a newly composed stay arrangement is established. Do not add spa, meal and flowers to create an invented package total.

## 9. Conversation and brief handoff

Explore the guest's priorities, room/family constraints, food and available time only where useful. Ask whether a celebration should be discreet; never assume champagne or flowers are welcome. Experience proposals go to concierge; room instructions through reception to housekeeping; dietary details to the kitchen; treatment suitability to the spa. Keep proposals and public entitlements distinct. No suggestions after personalisation refusal.

## 10. Review queue and evidence conflicts

Confirm concierge owner, exact room/cot/accessibility, pillow choices, menu season and allergy process, spa appointment/age rules and late-hour feasibility. Resolve parking inclusion versus dated tariff. Obtain current breakfast/flowers/transfer/romantic-offer pricing; don't revive the 2024 section. Public documents do not show adaptation authority, discretionary budgets or stock. No hotel contact or live booking check was made.
