# Hôtel Amour Nice — service DNA

## 1. Review status and use

Research checked: 5 October 2026. Public preparation; operator review pending; no operational confirmation from the hotel. Published prices are reference points, not quotes or inclusions. Source IDs connect facts to the register. Composition examples illustrate reasoning, not an exhaustive catalogue or authorised offers.

## 2. Identity and service interpretation

A 38-room, art-filled hotel at 3 avenue des Fleurs, with collected furniture, colourful interiors, a planted patio and a Greek taverna. The separate beach venue is at 47 promenade des Anglais. [A01, A03]

Interpretation: informal hospitality, food, visual discovery and seaside sociability can sit together. Ask whether the guest wants quiet, encounters or a mixture. Neither artwork nor a music programme establishes the guest's tastes; informal service still requires coordination.

## 4. Rooms, sleep and practical comfort

Small rooms are listed at 12 m²; medium at 16 m²; large at 16 or 25 m²; studios at 26 m² with a sitting area. Some rooms have balconies or bathtubs, not all. Rooms are described without television or telephone. Reception must check the actual booked room before suggesting a bath or particular layout. [A01]

No primary public pillow menu, mattress adjustment, cot policy, babysitting service, spa or in-room treatment programme was located. These remain unknown, not proven impossible. Accessibility routes, room preparation permissions, minibar and room-service scope need confirmation.

## 8. Composition possibilities — proposals for staff

| Volunteered guest cue | Known resources | Possible composition | Owner and checks |

Other combinations are welcome when supported by resources. Cross-venue ingredient use, private service, free attention and recipe changes remain proposals requiring approval.

## 9. Conversation and brief handoff

Start with purpose and desired rhythm. Explore food, social atmosphere, sea time or comfort only when relevant. Preserve the distinction between a taste preference and an allergy; staff verify recipes and cross-contact. Route experience ideas to the confirmed concierge or reception, room requests to housekeeping through reception, and food to the right venue. Each idea names guest cue, source, adjustment, timing and unresolved cost/permission. No suggestions after a refusal of personalisation.

## 10. Review queue and evidence conflicts

Before use: confirm handoff owner, season/rooftop, room/accessibility, current menus, bar hours and drink-PDF attribution, custom-drink authority and welcome budget. Obtain pillow/linen choices, cot/childcare rules, allergy process, room-service scope and beach tariffs. Brochures are dated 2025/2026; no live quote or stock check was made and no hotel was contacted.
