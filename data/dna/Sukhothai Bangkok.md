## 2. Identity and service interpretation

Thai architectural references, gardens, courtyards and lotus ponds coexist with destination dining, wellness, fitness and substantial event capability. [S01, S05, S16–S19, S21]

Interpretation: the hotel can support calm and privacy without being limited to passive stays. The current spa includes private boxing, training and yoga; events include large functions. Historical descriptions that excluded sport or substantial gatherings were too categorical. Ask what energy and interaction the guest wants.

## 4. Rooms, sleep and practical comfort

The renovated Main Wing Deluxe page describes teak, 300-thread-count cotton, Jim Thompson furnishings, room controls and bathroom amenities; connection to a Deluxe Suite is possible, not guaranteed. Room wing/category matters: do not merge renovated Main Wing and Club Wing specifications. [S02]

Club Lounge is published 06:30–22:00. Benefits include soft drinks, wake-up tea/coffee in room, two laundry pieces per stay, and a six-person meeting room for two hours per stay with reservation/availability. Breakfast 06:30–10:30 weekdays, until 11:00 weekends; afternoon tea 14:00–16:00; cocktails/canapés 17:30–19:30. Verify eligibility against booking. [S04]

A pillow menu appears in an OTA listing, but no current primary list of fillings or firmness was located. Older herbal/brand-specific pillow claims are not reused. Quiet location, hypoallergenic bedding, cot/connection/accessibility and scent-free preparation require room-specific confirmation. [S03]

## 8. Composition possibilities — proposals for staff

| Volunteered guest cue | Known resources | Possible composition | Owner and checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Likes ginger/lime, avoids alcohol and sugary drinks | Ginger/lime, juice, lotus tea and bar technique [S08, S20, S22] | Ask for a less sweet ginger–lime or tea-based welcome drink | Concierge + relevant bar: venue stock, recipe, timing, cross-outlet access and charge |
| Enjoys Thai food but avoids intense chilli | Celadon spice adaptation and vegetarian recipes [S05, S06] | A milder kitchen-selected Thai sequence around liked ingredients | Restaurant: spice versus allergy distinction, sauces, cross-contact, availability and price |
| Wants an active morning without a packed itinerary | Private boxing/yoga, pool, breakfast [S16, S19, S22] | One appropriately paced session followed by breakfast/free time | Spa + F&B: ability/suitability, age, slot, duration, food timing and charges |
| Has a short confidential working session | Entitled Club meeting room and wake-up drinks [S04] | Coordinate the included room window and refreshments | Concierge/Club team: entitlement, room availability, equipment, privacy and any extra cost |
| Food-focused celebration without alcohol | Chocolate preparation, pastry, Thai/Italian kitchens and soft drinks [S08, S13, S14] | Ask about a suitable food-led attention or sequence | F&B: actual date, dietary needs, off-schedule feasibility, setting and quote |
| Wants recovery but dislikes oil or strong fragrance | Oil-free Thai technique, adapted spa work [S19] | Therapist-led choice of technique and quieter timing | Spa: suitability, pressure, products, slot and price; do not promise a clinical benefit |

Any other combination may be explored from documented capabilities. A menu ingredient does not establish free access to another outlet's stock. No invented package price or guest-facing promise.

## 9. Conversation and brief handoff

Use purpose, energy, tastes and relevant practical needs. Avoid framing every guest as seeking silence or spiritual wellness. Do not recommend treatments based on inferred medical conditions. Ideas go to concierge/reception for coordination, with the actual outlet/team and conditions named. Preserve the booked wing/category and entitled benefits. A refusal of personalisation suppresses all experience suggestions.

## 10. Review queue and evidence conflicts

Confirm live menus, tax handling, brunch/Colonnade reopening, meeting layout/capacity and quoted billing basis. Obtain primary pillow list, room accessibility/connection/cot details, housekeeping permissions, adaptation budgets, allergy procedures and current handoff owners. Clarify spa cancellation and any family-access differences. Historical staff, partner lists and blanket “no sport/no events” rules are not current facts. No hotel contact or live booking was made.
