# Golden Well / U Zlaté Studně — service DNA

## 1. Review status and use

Research checked: 5 October 2026. Public preparation; operator review pending; no hotel operational confirmation. Prices retain their published conditions. Compositions are proposals for staff, not promises or a closed catalogue.

## 2. Identity and service interpretation

A small historic hotel below Prague Castle: 17 rooms and two suites, period furniture, artwork and garden/city views. Restaurant terraces include two outdoors and one indoors. The approach is traffic-free and cobbled. [G01]

Interpretation: room detail, private guiding and destination dining support a personally paced stay. Ask whether history, food, a celebration, work or rest matters most. Do not assign a romantic personality or assume that the approach is accessible to everyone.

## 6. Wellness, activities and family resources

In-room massage and bath preparation are published; a full spa, pool, sauna or gym is not established. Staff must confirm therapist, oils, fragrance preference, room space and fees. [G06]

Private guiding can address architecture/history, with a three-hour walk in one package. Match pace, language, route and weather. Children/extra-bed rules do not prove childcare or a kids' programme. [G06, G09, G12]

## 8. Composition possibilities — proposals for staff

| Volunteered guest cue | Known resources | Possible composition | Owner and checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Avoids feather pillows, values sleep | Bedding menu [G02] | Appropriate non-down setup and agreed turndown timing | Reception + housekeeping: fill, firmness, stock, allergy process; no clinical guarantee |
| Anniversary without alcohol or public fuss | Alcohol-free pairing/sparkling, pralines, turndown [G06, G10, G11, G14] | Discreet praline and alcohol-free attention instead of wine/petals | Reception + restaurant: substitution permission, delivery, cost, consent |
| Architecture interest but low walking stamina | Tailored guide, lounge, room breakfast [G06–G08, G12] | Shorter/slower walk with a break and unhurried breakfast | Reception: route, demands, language, availability, revised price |
| Enjoys food but dislikes long dinners | Restaurant expertise [G10] | Ask about a shorter seasonal sequence | Restaurant: service constraints, diet and quote; shorter menu not published |
| Wants relaxation without scent | Bath and in-room massage [G06] | Unscented treatment or simple bath preparation | Reception + therapist + housekeeping: products, suitability, room/bath, timing and fee |

Packages demonstrate coordinated resources, not unlimited permission to recombine them. Other evidence-based compositions remain possible.

## 9. Conversation and brief handoff

Explore relevant sleep comfort, walking pace, food and degree of ceremony without a feature questionnaire. Experiences go to the confirmed concierge/reception; bedding/bath setup through reception to housekeeping; food to restaurant. Each suggestion links a volunteered cue, evidence, proposed adjustment and outstanding checks. A refusal of personalisation overrides every example.

## 10. Review queue and evidence conflicts

Obtain actual pillow menu, room-specific access/bath/connecting options, breakfast/à-la-carte menus, therapist/bath tariffs and dietary controls. Resolve Emperor breakfast wording and extra-bed billing unit. Confirm garden access and current chef/menu. Restaurant tabs did not reliably expose current breakfast/à-la-carte documents, so missing details were not inferred. No availability check or hotel contact was made.
