## 2. Identity and service interpretation

A beachfront Lombok resort with spacious suites, contemporary art/design, food, spa and activity coordination. The official site supports both private accommodation and shared beach/pool spaces. [V01–V05]

An April 2025 first-hand report describes 24 suites, a DJ booth/beach-club atmosphere and seasonal or private use of Majesty. It is dated external observation, not a current entertainment programme or operating policy. [V14]

Interpretation: do not inherit the historical profile's narrow assumption that only quiet couples fit. Privacy, social energy, food and activity may coexist. Ask which balance the guest wants and confirm the actual programme. Do not classify sports, families or small professional gatherings as inherently incompatible without an explicit constraint.

## 5. Food and drink resources

The official dining page establishes Indonesian/international cuisine, seafood, wine, cocktails and location flexibility. No reliable current full food, cocktail, breakfast or allergen price menu was found through the official site and targeted document searches. Do not replace that gap with a generic tropical-fruit inventory. [V05]

The dated independent report names Laksa Bogor, Octopus Taliwang and Sate Rembiga, reports vegetarian/vegan breakfast choices and a welcome-drink choice. These show historical culinary direction only; recipes, ingredients, stock, present menu and prices require confirmation. [V14]

The spa page mentions roselle tea before and ginger tea after one treatment, plus fresh avocado/aloe in a wrap. This establishes spa use at the time of publication, not food-grade stock at the bar or permission to move spa ingredients into drinks. The tea practice is a useful lead for an alcohol-free welcome preference, subject to spa/F&B coordination. [V06]

The historical pre-arrival form's vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free options show that such requests were collected. They do not certify recipes, cross-contact controls or every requested substitution. Obtain the actual menus and allergy process before turning dietary requests into execution instructions.

## 6. Wellness, activities and family resources

Spa hours are published as 10:00–22:00; advance reservation is requested. The page lists a 90-minute facial, an avocado/aloe wrap and an aromatic flower/oil bath; standalone prices are not published in the reviewed material. Product branding on the page should be rechecked before naming a current brand to a guest. [V06]

The child treatment is 45 minutes for ages 6–13, residents only, with an adult present for under-10s. This does not establish a kids' club or babysitter. Fitness is published 07:00–22:00 and for ages 16+. [V06, V07]

Activities page describes tailored catamaran routes/duration/vessels, diving with a claimed PADI centre, helicopter arrangements and golf coordination. The website's reef-distance claim is not a safety assessment. Ask staff about current certified operator, age/ability requirements, sea conditions, transfers, equipment and cancellation. No current activity tariff was verified. [V08]

## 9. Conversation and brief handoff

Use the guest's purpose, desired privacy/social balance, food preferences and appetite for activity. Don't lead with honeymoon packages or ask a long activity checklist. Collect relevant swim/boat comfort as a preference, not medical profiling. Experiences go to confirmed concierge or reception/guest services; room requests to housekeeping; food to F&B; treatments and water activity suitability to professionals. No proposals after personalisation refusal.

## 10. Review queue and evidence conflicts

Historical exclusions on sports/events and dated guest-review staff names are not current operational constraints. Secondary pillow/amenity claims require primary confirmation. No hotel contact or booking check was made.
