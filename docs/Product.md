# Canopia and the hackathon experiment

Canopia helps a hotel understand the people behind a reservation. A voluntary pre-arrival conversation explores wishes, individual tastes, relationships, routines, comfort and practical constraints. The hotel's DNA informs what the host explores and how the resulting brief turns that understanding into useful preparation, adaptations, personal touches and relevant paid-service ideas.

The guest does not have to design the hotel's attention. The hotel contributes its identity, expertise, resources and operational judgement. An ingredient, package or room feature can support a new proposal without proving that a specific adaptation is available, included or approved.

The existing application has one local operator console. It manages stays across six hotels, separate guest links, DNA snapshots, conversation, automatic draft generation, human editing, saved versions and text export. Experience proposals belong to the concierge, or reception where appropriate. Final formatting and delivery are manual. Real guest operation remains disabled.

## The additional value to test

Context enrichment researches public information connected to an interest the guest already declared. Local discovery finds relevant activities, venues or events for the stay. Both should lead to better hotel-reviewed ideas than conversation plus DNA alone. They may run independently or contribute to the same proposal.

The comparison keeps the conversation, dates and DNA fixed. Preserve the original brief, show what evidence was added, and explain which proposals a human retained, edited or rejected. Finding nothing useful is a valid outcome. The result should make clear whether a proposal changes preparation, a personal touch or a paid experience.

## Evidence boundaries

The six profiles were assembled from public sources on 5 October 2026. They are not hotel-approved operating instructions or current inventories. Their sources, conditions and unanswered questions remain in each profile.

The three examples are actual saved model outputs on fictional scenarios, not real guests. Their brief-v4.2 outputs have known omissions and a routing-heading error. The v5 conversations include long pauses and repetitive turns; the later v5.1 prompt was checked on targeted replays upstream. This repository does not claim a newly validated live-model journey.

Scripted demo conversation and simulated research are different from those recorded examples. They enable local workflow development without costs and cannot demonstrate hospitality quality or external factual accuracy.
